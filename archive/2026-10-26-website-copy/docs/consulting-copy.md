# I help founders build and launch the right custom software

From deciding what to build first to getting it into your customers’ hands, I take ownership of the technical side so you can focus on building your business.

## You have a clear vision. You need someone you can trust to build it

You’ve done the groundwork and know custom software is the next step, but you need a clear path from your vision to a product customers can use. Your business depends on getting this built well, and hiring a developer can feel like a leap of faith when you’re relying on their technical judgement. Every week without a product delays your chance to reach customers and start earning revenue.

## We start by making sure we’re building the right thing

Building software is expensive. Building the wrong thing costs even more. We’ll be ruthless about essentials, deciding what belongs in the first release and what can wait. Each feature should support a clear business goal, keeping the scope focused so you can launch sooner and spend less.

AI lets me move at the speed of a small team. My job is to make sure that speed goes toward software your business needs, built to hold up with real customers.

## How we’ll take your product from idea to launch

### Discovery

_Timeline: **Free • by the end of week 1**_

We work through your business goals, who the product needs to serve, and what the first release should accomplish. I document those decisions and create an initial roadmap, giving us a shared understanding of what we’re building and why.

### Planning & Design

_Timeline: **by week 4**_

I turn the roadmap into a prioritized plan, working through how the product should behave and resolving important questions before development begins. Wireframes, flow sketches, and interface examples help you review the direction and make decisions before we commit time to building.

Planning & Design is a paid phase with its own agreed budget. By the end, you’ll have a defined scope and a build estimate, so you can decide how to proceed.

### Base Platform Configuration

_Timeline: **by week 6**_

I configure and deploy the services your product needs, such as hosting, authentication, payments, email, and monitoring. We select these around your project’s requirements, with each setup item clearly priced in advance.

### Custom Development Cycles

_Timeline: **week 6 onward**_

We work in repeating development cycles. Each starts with a review of the previous cycle and an agreement on what comes next. I plan and build the agreed work, then release it to a private environment for you to review.

New ideas and requirements go onto the backlog for a future cycle, keeping the current work focused. Your feedback helps us decide what to tackle next.

## Technical support as your business evolves

Once your product is live, you’ll have customers to learn from, improvements to make, and software to keep running reliably. Whether you need continued development or occasional help with updates and fixes, I’ll be there to help.

As your business grows, we’ll work out what technical support makes sense. That might mean continuing together, expanding the team, or handing the work over to someone new. I can help other developers get up to speed and take on responsibility, so the product keeps moving forward.

In 2017, I teamed up with Tap Bio’s two founders to build a mini-website platform for people and businesses on Instagram. Early customers included Eminem, Celine Dion, and Fender. We scaled to millions of page views, and the platform ran for nine years, until October 2026.

### Yardly

I joined Yardly as the technical lead in 2015, working with the founders to define the company’s direction and build its foundational platform: a home services marketplace focused on lawn care and snow removal. The company was acquired in 2022.

## Who I work best with

I work best directly with founders who have a clear business vision and are ready to commit time and money to bringing it to life. They want an experienced technical partner who helps make decisions and takes responsibility for the build.

You’ll bring your knowledge of your customers and business and stay involved as we set priorities and review progress. I’ll own the technical work while you focus on reaching customers, generating sales, and running the business.

For a new product built from scratch, plan on a budget of $50,000 USD or more. We’ll first discuss whether your goals and budget are a good fit, then define the scope and estimate the build cost through paid planning and design.
