## We start by making sure we’re building the right thing

Building software is expensive. Building the wrong thing costs even more. We’ll be ruthless about essentials, deciding what belongs in the first release and what can wait. Each feature should support a clear business goal, keeping the scope focused on <mark>what matters for launch</mark> and getting you there sooner.

AI lets me move at the speed of a small team. My job is to make sure that speed goes toward software your business needs, built to hold up with real customers.
