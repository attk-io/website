## Technical support as your business evolves

Once your product is live, you’ll have customers to learn from, improvements to make, and software to keep running reliably. Whether you need continued development or occasional help with updates and fixes, <mark>I’ll be there to help</mark>.

As your business grows, we’ll work out what technical support makes sense. That might mean continuing together, expanding the team, or handing the work over to someone new. I can help other developers get up to speed and take on responsibility, so the product keeps moving forward.
