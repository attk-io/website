## I’m Christian. You’ll work directly with me

I’m an independent software engineer from Canada. Since 2016, I’ve worked with founders to build and launch software their businesses depend on.

You’ll work with me from the first planning conversation through design, development, and launch. I make the technical decisions, build the product, and keep you involved along the way, so you always know who’s responsible and where things stand.

You can expect straightforward advice and room to ask questions. I’ll explain the tradeoffs in plain language, tell you when I think an idea needs rethinking, and be clear about what I recommend and why. You bring the knowledge of your business; I’ll help you make informed decisions about the software behind it.
