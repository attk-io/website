# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14

## 1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit attk.io, a website operated by ATTK Software Inc ("we," "us," or "our"), an Alberta corporation, and how that information is used. The site is informational: it has no accounts, and you aren't asked to enter information on the site itself.

## 2. Information We Collect

**When you visit the site**, we collect information about how you use it through Google Analytics (see Section 4). The services that deliver the site also receive standard technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, in order to load the page for you (see Section 5).

**When you book a call or email us**, we receive the information you choose to share. The "Book a short introduction call" link takes you to a booking page hosted by Google Calendar, where you provide your name, email address, and any other details you include. If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use this information only to:

- Respond to your message and schedule and hold the call you booked
- Understand, in aggregate, how visitors use the site so we can improve it

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties, except to the service providers described in Section 5.

## 4. Analytics and Cookies

We use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google, to understand how visitors find and use the site. Google Analytics uses cookies (small files stored by your browser) to collect information such as:

- The pages you visit and how long you spend on them
- The website or search that referred you to us
- Your browser, device type, and screen size
- Your approximate location (such as country or city), derived from your IP address

Google Analytics does not log or store your IP address. We look at this information only in aggregate and do not use it to identify individual visitors.

You can opt out of Google Analytics by blocking cookies in your browser settings or by installing the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). The site will continue to work normally if you do.

The site also stores a single value in your browser's local storage to remember that you've already seen the opening animation. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.

## 5. Service Providers

The site relies on the following services, all provided by Google:

- **Firebase Hosting** serves the website
- **Google Fonts** delivers the site's typefaces, so your browser requests them directly from Google's servers
- **Google Analytics** measures site usage, as described in Section 4
- **Google Calendar** hosts the booking page for introduction calls

Each of these services processes information under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), and may store it on servers outside Canada, including in the United States. For more about Google Analytics specifically, see [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 6. Data Retention

We keep booking details and emails only for as long as needed to respond to you and to keep ordinary business records. Analytics data is kept according to the data retention settings in our Google Analytics account, after which it is deleted by Google.

## 7. Your Rights

You can ask us to:

- Tell you what personal information we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate information
- Delete your information, subject to legal requirements

You can also opt out of analytics at any time, as described in Section 4.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

## 9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or want to make a request about your information, contact us at christian@attk.io.
