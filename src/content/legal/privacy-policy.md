# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14

## 1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit attk.io, a website operated by ATTK Software Inc ("we," "us," or "our"), an Alberta corporation, and how that information is used. ATTK Software Inc is responsible for (the "controller" of) this information. The site is informational: it has no accounts, and you aren't asked to enter information on the site itself.

## 2. Information We Collect

**When you visit the site**, the service that hosts it receives standard technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, in order to load the page for you (see Section 5). We also count visits using analytics that don't use cookies (see Section 4).

**When you book a call or email us**, we receive the information you choose to share. The "Book a short introduction call" link takes you to a booking page hosted by Google Calendar, where you provide your name, email address, and any other details you include. If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message.

You don't have to give us any personal information to use the site. If you'd like to book a call, we need your name and email address to schedule it.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use this information only for the purposes below. For visitors in the European Union, the United Kingdom, and other places with similar laws, we've also listed the legal basis we rely on for each.

- **To deliver the site and keep it secure.** Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a working, secure website.
- **To understand, in aggregate, how visitors use the site so we can improve it.** Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the site, using analytics designed not to identify you (see Section 4).
- **To respond to your message and to schedule and hold the call you booked.** Legal basis: taking steps you've asked for before possibly entering into a contract with us, and our legitimate interest in replying to inquiries.

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties, except to the service providers described in Section 5. We don't use your information for automated decision-making or profiling.

## 4. Analytics and Cookies

This site doesn't use cookies, and it doesn't store anything else on your device.

We measure site usage with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It doesn't use cookies or local storage, and it doesn't fingerprint visitors or track them over time. When a page loads, a small script sends Cloudflare information such as:

- The page you visited
- The website or search that referred you to us
- Your browser, operating system, and device type
- Your country, derived from your IP address
- How quickly the page loaded

We see this information only as aggregate reports, such as total page views and top referring sites.

If you'd rather not be counted, you can block static.cloudflareinsights.com with a content blocker (most ad blockers already do). The site will continue to work normally if you do.

The booking page for introduction calls is run by Google, so Google's own privacy and cookie practices apply once you follow that link.

## 5. Service Providers

The site relies on the following services:

- **Firebase Hosting**, provided by Google, serves the website
- **Cloudflare Web Analytics**, provided by Cloudflare, measures site usage, as described in Section 4
- **Google Calendar**, provided by Google, hosts the booking page for introduction calls
- **Fastmail** handles our email

Each of these services processes information under its own privacy policy: [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/), and [Fastmail](https://www.fastmail.com/policies/privacy/).

## 6. International Transfers

We're based in Canada, and our service providers may store and process information outside Canada, including in the United States.

For visitors in the European Union, the European Commission recognizes Canada as providing adequate protection for personal information. Where information is transferred to the United States, it's protected by the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework (and its UK Extension), under which Google and Cloudflare are certified, or by Standard Contractual Clauses in our providers' data processing terms.

## 7. Data Retention

- **Booking details and emails** are kept only for as long as needed to respond to you and to keep ordinary business records.
- **Analytics data** is available to us for up to six months.
- **Technical information** received by our hosting provider is kept according to Google's retention practices.

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to:

- Tell you what personal information we hold about you, and give you a copy
- Correct inaccurate information
- Delete your information, subject to legal requirements
- Restrict how we use your information
- Stop using your information where we rely on our legitimate interests
- Send your information to you or to another organization in a portable format

To make a request, contact us using the details in Section 10. We'll respond within 30 days. To avoid being counted in analytics, see Section 4.

If you're not satisfied with how we've handled your information, you can complain to a data protection authority. In Canada, that's the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. In the European Union or the United Kingdom, it's the data protection authority where you live or work. We'd appreciate the chance to address your concern first.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
