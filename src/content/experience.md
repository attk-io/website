## Experience building, launching, and growing software businesses

<div class="hidden py-6 sm:block"></div>

### [FundFilter ![](/logos/fundfilter.svg)](https://www.linkedin.com/company/fundfilter/)

I joined FundFilter in 2020 to help build a private fund data and analytics platform. In 2022, FundFilter was acquired by PEI Group. I supported the acquisition by fulfilling technical due diligence requests and staying on to help transition the product into PEI’s systems.

### [Tap Bio ![](/logos/tap-bio.svg)](https://www.linkedin.com/company/tapbio/)

In 2017, I teamed up with Tap Bio’s two founders to build a mini-website platform for people and businesses on Instagram. Early customers included Eminem, Celine Dion, and Fender. We scaled to millions of page views, and the platform ran for nine years, until October 2026.

### [Yardly ![](/logos/yardly.svg)](https://edmonton.taproot.news/news/2022/09/13/exit-leaves-yardly-co-founder-eager-to-start-again)

I joined Yardly as the technical lead in 2015, working with the founders to define the company’s direction and build its foundational platform: a home services marketplace focused on lawn care and snow removal. The company was acquired in 2022.
