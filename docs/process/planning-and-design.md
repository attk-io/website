# Planning & Design

Planning & Design is the second paid engagement after a successful Discovery. The goal is to turn the MVP scope from Discovery into build-ready detail: a full backlog, resolved flows, and enough UI direction to build consistently.

## Format

Planning & Design is primarily independent execution — I work through the artifacts without the client present, and loop them in when a decision is blocked or a course correction is needed. Ad-hoc calls handle realignment; there is no fixed meeting cadence.

The engagement typically runs one to two weeks, depending on the scope and complexity agreed during Discovery.

What I work through:

- Break each feature area into concrete behavior, edge cases, states, and constraints.
- Write issues and organize them into a prioritized backlog.
- Draft the Project Arc document.
- Produce any additional deliverables agreed during Discovery.

## Outcomes

The outcomes of Planning & Design are the artifacts that create overall plan and set the expectations of what the upcoming development cycles will focus on.

**Project Arc**

The client's specific Project Arc document — the named checkpoints that map this project's progression from current state through each meaningful release and beyond. Shaped by the outputs of all preceding checkpoints.

**Product Roadmap**

This takes the shape of Issues organized into a prioritized backlog. Feature areas from Discovery are broken into concrete issues — covering behavior, edge cases, states, and constraints — and organized into a prioritized backlog ready for the development cycle to run against.

**Additional deliverables**

Any artifacts agreed during Discovery as part of the [Planning & Design scope](../process/discovery.md). Common examples:

- wireframes of key user flows
- hi-fidelity examples of component UI
- a product branding guide
