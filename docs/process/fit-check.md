# Fit Check

A Fit Check is the first conversation with a prospective client. The client talks a little about their project and business; I explain how I work, where I tend to be useful, what the relationship asks of them, and what next step makes sense. The goal is to decide whether to continue into Discovery, talk about a different working relationship, or end the conversation here.

## Format

- A single ~30-minute video call is the default.
- Async (email or shared doc) works when scheduling is hard, time zones don't line up, or the client thinks better in writing. The same ground gets covered; the outcome is the same.
- If a synchronous call has happened, I write up a short summary afterward and send it across, so both sides have the same record of what was discussed and decided.
- No NDA required by default — if the client needs one, a lightweight mutual NDA can be signed before the call.

Useful prompts:

- What are you building?
- What stage is it at?
- What made you reach out now?
- What kind of help do you think you need?
- Are there any hard constraints around timing, budget, or existing commitments?

What I cover:

- How I work with non-technical founders.
- Where I am strongest.
- What the client needs to bring for the relationship to work.
- What happens after the Fit Check if they continue.

## Outcomes

**If Discovery is the right next step:**

- Agreement to begin a Discovery engagement.
- A rough time budget for Discovery (two to three working days) and a target start window.
- An understanding of the broad shape of the project, enough for Discovery to be set up usefully.
- A short written follow-up confirming the above, sent within a day or two of the call.

**If a different relationship might fit better:**

- A clear read that Discovery is not the right next step.
- The kind of relationship that might make sense instead — code review, senior advisory, technical due diligence, architecture review, or another bounded engagement.
- A short written follow-up naming the proposed shape, so the next conversation is about whether to start that work rather than continuing the Fit Check.

**If the engagement ends here:**

- An honest read on why — wrong stage, wrong domain, timeline / budget mismatch, model mismatch, or a problem I'm not the right person for.
- Where possible, a pointer to a more appropriate option: a different kind of partner, a no-code path, an agency, a hire profile, or a different sequencing of the work.
- The client leaves with a clearer articulation of what they're building, even if the answer is "not with me" — the intake questions tend to be useful regardless of the outcome.
